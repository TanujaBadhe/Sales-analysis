# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard — Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a Tableau-based data analytics project designed to analyze sales performance and identify important business trends.

The project focuses on **sales, revenue, profit, products, customers, regions, and categories**, providing an interactive view of business performance through Tableau dashboards.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Find top-performing products and categories.
* Analyze regional sales performance.
* Understand customer segments.
* Compare sales and profitability.
* Identify areas with strong and weak performance.
* Present insights through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

| Total Profit     | Total profit generated   |
| Total Quantity   | Total units sold         |

* Sales and profit trends over time.
